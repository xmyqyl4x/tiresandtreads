# P2-PR-014 — Local private tire-photo processing and confirmation

The owner authorized local completion, full verification and merge of P2-PR-012–014 while actual S1/S2 and existing acceptance/deployment decisions remain held. This card is an opt-in backend factory. It adds no HTTP/frontend endpoint, default worker dispatch, production composition, role bootstrap, CLI/preflight or policy/migration promotion. The service role is a trusted backend capability, not an end-user credential or an authentication substitute.

## Upload authority, limits and quarantine

The owner first obtains an idempotent server-issued capture group for the assessment's optional photo input. Upload intents must reference that current owned group and its reviewed photo policy. Arbitrary or foreign batch IDs are rejected. A reviewed plan is mandatory and bounds daily captures, daily image count/bytes and concurrent pending uploads. The database serializes admission on the owner root; four total images are allowed per capture, including previously completed/deleted images. Starting another capture represents another assessment and still consumes the same owner's daily limits. No actual operating quota or provider budget is selected by fixture values.

Each intent captures explicit processing permission, the current subject revision, notice/evidence, a private original and sanitized asset identity, MIME/checksum/byte length and an independently hashed ten-minute grant. Commands bind their entire body and policy/codec version. The normal image lifetime is at most 24 hours. Exact finalize replay returns current status without another write. Foreign/share-like owner keys, forged/expired grants, changed bytes or MIME/magic mismatches cannot finalize. API-specific IP/CSRF/body limits and browser assessment binding belong to the held P2 API/frontend cards; these backend checks do not replace them.

Originals enter encrypted versioned private storage under pending quarantine metadata. Asset reads do not expose them as ready or public. The required scanner port fails closed on unsafe, unavailable or timed-out results. Its transport has a five-second abortable limit and no default clean adapter. A separate child process decodes JPEG/PNG/WebP with a 40-megapixel input ceiling, rejects animated/multipage images, applies orientation, downscales inside 4,096 pixels, flattens transparency and re-encodes JPEG without EXIF/GPS or other source metadata. Input/output are capped at ten MiB, libvips processing at five seconds and the parent process at ten seconds. Two decoder children are admitted per process/module; saturation falls back. The child receives no inherited provider credentials. Heap limits do not claim an independent operating-system sandbox or native-memory isolation; actual hostile-image/AV acceptance remains held.

The intent's exclusive finalization lease and scope/subject/image barriers are rechecked around storage and codec work. Only complete, verified original/sanitized versions become a ready owned upload. Failed scanning/decoding rejects and hides the intent, and uses replayable private cleanup. A crashed pending write can be adopted only through the existing identical-byte/version/encryption checks; a conflicting asset is never overwritten.

## Extraction and explicit confirmation

Only a sanitized, version-bound JPEG and pinned policy configuration reach the extraction transport. There are no original images, owner/session/contact data, arbitrary image URLs, tools or provider storage in that contract. Its strict bounded output describes at most one candidate per supported field, a fixed quality state, unknown reason and optional normalized image region. Input/output token ceilings are 1,048,576/1,024, with an actual 25-second transport deadline. These conservative contract bounds must be evaluated against a real selected provider before activation.

The common runner reuses P2-PR-013's reviewed cost admission, per-attempt accounting, bounded known-cost retries, uncertainty holds, circuit generations and durable job/reuse fences. Calls occur outside SQL; cost settlement survives a refused or revoked publication. An optional runner start/failure hook seals the photo job lease and restores the manual path after terminal failure. Artifact retention/validity is bounded by the image lifetime, rather than the ordinary thirty-day report-artifact cap. Artifact, sealed extraction and candidate rows publish atomically; deleting or withdrawing while the provider returns prevents publication and preserves known charges.

Brand/model, tire size, load/speed markings, DOT/date fragments and seasonal/run-flat markings remain suggestions. Low-quality/invalid readings become unknown; ambiguous/duplicate/extra output is refused. Partial DOT text remains partial, with no invented manufacture date or full TIN. No photo determines tire condition, safety, fitment approval, authenticity or recall clearance. Quality labels are not calibrated model probabilities. The real specialist/labeled-image evaluation remains P2-PR-017.

Accept/correct/manual commands require the current owner and expected field revision. They use the same ordinary domain shape validator and append-only confirmation repository, deriving accepted/corrected origin from the selected current candidate. Foreign candidates, unknown candidates and concurrent stale revisions fail. Non-metric tire codes retain the ordinary manual path with unknown/unverified dimensions. Confirmation returns explicit values for a later ordinary report/refine command; no report facts, ranking or safety conclusions are automatically changed. Manual entry needs no image or model call.

## Removal, verification and remaining integration

Removal commits immediate scope denial and cancels active extraction before storage cleanup. Reads, confirmations and late publication are then refused, including during a cleanup outage. A recorded cleanup barrier waits beyond an in-flight finalization lease and bounded storage write before erasing exact-key versions. Replays drain all versions/markers before marking assets and intent deleted. Current legal holds defer physical erasure. A bounded maintenance operation processes up to 25 expired/deleting records. Controlled timestamp tests prove barrier decisions; they do not claim that real time elapsed or that a provider accepted cancellation.
