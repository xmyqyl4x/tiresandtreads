## Aggregate privacy and freshness

Internal insights count distinct permitted visitors in disjoint first-observed web/app channel cells per completed UTC week. Partner figures count distinct permitted recipients in an accepted-send cohort; outcomes are as observed at `computedAt`, not claims that every outcome occurred within that week. Daily distinct counts are never added to invent monthly unique visitors. Counts use exact integer strings; unknown is not zero.

There are no caller-selected dimensions, date ranges, total rows, drilldowns, moving windows, consent overrides, per-offer slices or exports. A positive cell and its positive complement must each have at least **50** people, and the whole cohort must have at least 50. Otherwise the entire matrix, including any potential total, is suppressed. A released/suppressed audience/week is immutable: repeat requests return its original timestamp/values rather than exposing changing marginal counts. Late intake cannot refresh it. Corrections or privacy invalidation permanently block that period; no less-one replacement can be requested. This conservative restriction intentionally trades availability for protection. Cross-organization collusion/deidentification, external Tier A sale and contractual controls are not accepted by these local safeguards.

Source scans are bounded at 10,000 candidate rows per audience/week and refuse overflow instead of silently truncating. Lists are bounded at 50, with actor/purpose/week-bound continuations for visitors. Dedicated pools, statement/transaction deadlines, stable resource ordering and an accepted-send index bound local work. These are conservative local admission limits, not an approved production SLA or a claim that arbitrary high-volume analytical workloads have been accepted.
