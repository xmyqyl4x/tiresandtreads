## Layers and callable boundary

`packages/engine/src/pricing` owns version `1.0.0` arithmetic, quote construction, comparisons and ownership scenarios. It imports only pure domain values and engine types/helpers: no SQL, SDK, network, environment, implicit clock, randomness, advertising or UI. All monetary computations use nonnegative signed-64-bit-bounded integer USD minor units; intervals have ordered low/high bounds. Rates are explicit decimal strings with at most six places. No exchange rate, tax jurisdiction or fee default is guessed. Unsupported currencies, package quantities, dates, scopes and cyclic fee bases fail with a redacted value error.

`createPricingService({now, evidence}).estimate(unknown)` accepts only quote ID, nullable plan ID, region key and explicit alignment selection. It normalizes the request, reads one injected clock and invokes the trusted `PricingEvidence.load` application port. ID/plan/region/alignment/time mismatch is an integrity failure; adapter exceptions are redacted. Raw client prices, rights, authorization flags, source declarations and commercial terms cannot enter this service contract.

The **019 resolver** must load actual immutable quote/product/assumption records through the accepted repositories, authorize private owner quotes, verify current licenses/published bundle/region/tax/inclusion policy and compile `QuoteInput` on the server. A private shop quote has `origin: owner_quote`, no fabricated feed license and an authorization decision supplied by that trusted resolver. Retailer, product, expiry and tax-context identities can remain unknown. The pure function's authorization flag is a compiled fact, never proof of authorization by itself. Licensed feeds require a current scoped catalog source with all required rights. No production resolver or HTTP/UI adapter is fabricated here.

Quote and scenario estimates carry `mathVersion: 1.0.0` and `issuanceAuthorized: false`. Before report issuance, 019 must preserve the exact input snapshot, quote and component IDs, life-model version, assumption-set identity, explicit clock and result. An assumption ID alone cannot make an arbitrary client estimate trusted or reproducible. Future web, app and MCP transports call the same services after S4 acceptance.

## Quote and installed-total rules

| Concern | Implemented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Selection | One, two or four tires with explicit front/rear SKU quantities; missing SKU identity is unverified. Each price line covers only requested items, without duplicate or excess quantity |
| Price basis | Per tire multiplies by tire count; per axle requires complete two-tire axle quantities; a set/package applies only to its declared exact count. A package is never silently divided for a partial replacement |
| Scope | One coherent quote, bundle, region, retailer, currency, timestamp, tax context and assumption version. Bounds describe that source's low/high scenarios; the function never assembles a cheapest cart from incompatible retailers |
| Required services | Each line needs explicit mounting, balancing, TPMS/valve, disposal and tax coverage. `included` adds zero again; a confirmed zero remains zero. Missing components or prices leave installed total null rather than becoming free |
| Tax | Line tax-inclusion flags must agree with tax components. Explicit percentage bases can include named required fees; referenced coverage must be within the charge's scope. Duplicate/missing references and cycles fail. Per-line versus per-order half-up rounding is explicit. Ambiguous per-line allocation of aggregate fees is rejected |
| Alignment | Separately reported unless requested. A tax base cannot pull unselected alignment into the required total. Requested alignment requires explicit coverage |
| Freshness | Less than 48 hours is fresh; 48 hours to less than seven days is qualified stale evidence with no current-price claim. Seven days or offer expiry hides monetary values. Missing rights/current source or denied owner authorization also hides values. These thresholds are project policy, not provider guarantees |
| Unknown product | Authorized quote arithmetic can be shown with an unverified-product reason, but cannot establish fitment, enter price winners or bypass safety. A null tax-context identity also prevents price comparison |

Synthetic arithmetic fixture: four tires at $150 plus $100 of declared services and an explicit fixture-only 7% tax on $700 yields **$749 installed**. This rate is not tax advice or a geographic default. Fee, quantity, rate and aggregate overflow checks preserve exact values beyond JavaScript's safe-integer limit.

## Comparison and recommendation integration

`compareShopQuote` returns independent fit, climate/strategy and price findings. A price delta requires an eligible/suitable product, identical selected SKUs and axle quantities, the same fresh as-of/bundle/region/currency/tax/alignment/assumption context and known selected cost basis. Even merchandise comparison requires known installed inclusions. Unknowns yield `not_comparable`; the result has no invented fairness score and never labels a price unfair. Above/below/overlap relates only to the supplied observed range, not the whole market.

`orderPriceOptions` considers at most 100 unique quotes and filters out blocked/unverified products and unusable prices. Eligible products may have different SKUs but must share quantity/axle and cost context. Midpoint ordering is an explicit deterministic presentation convention with identity ties; a best-price highlight requires interval dominance over every other candidate. Overlapping ranges do not manufacture a winner, and a lone usable quote receives no best-price claim. Paid terms do not enter any arithmetic or ordering contract.

`attachExactPrice` is the conservative bridge to the [017 engine](P1-PR-017-eligibility-recommendations.md): match plan, selected replacement SKUs/axle quantities, bundle, region and assessment clock; attach only a fresh known licensed exact installed total. Ranges, stale values, private quotes or unknown products clear the existing point-cost field. **017's budget contract remains point-only:** range comparisons are available here, but no midpoint is used to claim compliance with a hard budget. A future explicitly versioned range-aware budget contract can broaden that behavior. 019 must compile/recheck prices at every assessment; an earlier estimate is not cached issuance authority.

## Cost per distance and ownership scenarios

`modeledCostPer1000` requires a current installed cost and a current reviewed SKU-specific **modeled-life** interval. Warranty mileage is a separate fact and cannot substitute for modeled life. Miles and kilometers normalize exactly to integer nanometers, retaining six-decimal input precision. Cost-per-distance bounds round outward to minor units; they express supported modeled intervals rather than extra decimal precision.

`estimateScenario` supports one year-round set or summer/winter sets over an explicit one-to-20-year horizon. Required inputs include eligibility, annual distance, winter basis-point share, wheel costs, additional swap count and price, annual storage, current reviewed mileage/calendar models and an explicit zero or reviewed residual assumption. Missing inputs produce an unavailable total with reasons, never an invented zero. Current implementation models full four-tire sets; partial quote totals are supported but partial-life forecasting remains unavailable.

For horizon distance `D`, summer distance is `D × (10000 − winterShare) / 10000` and winter distance is `D × winterShare / 10000`. The internal numerator/denominator preserves exact allocation even at micro-mile boundaries; the two sets never each consume all annual mileage. Per-set purchase bounds are `max(1, ceil(allocatedDistance / modeledLife), ceil(years / calendarYears))`. An exact boundary does not buy a needless extra set; exceeding it does. Stored seasonal tires still age in this explicit financial model. Purchase counts are bounded at 1,000.

The low scenario uses supported high life and low prices; the high scenario uses low life and high prices. Wheel cost is added once per set; initial installation is already in each purchased set's installed cost. Additional swap events exclude that initial installation, storage multiplies by years and explicit residual ranges subtract without going negative. Zero annual distance may still model age-related expenditure, but cost per distance remains null. Outputs retain purchase counts, life-model IDs, exact distance allocation, assumptions and missing-evidence reasons.

Version one explicitly assumes uniform annual use, constant current nominal prices, no inflation/discounting and the supplied residual policy. These are financial what-if assumptions, not future price forecasts, optimal replacement timing or safe-life dates. `compareScenarios` requires matching horizon, clock, region, currency, assumption-set ID, annual distance, winter share and residual policy; otherwise it returns no savings delta. 019 must verify that the assumption set really binds the common operating/tax/service policy and retain all constituent snapshots.
