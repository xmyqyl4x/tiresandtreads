## Pause, investigate and recover

Stop expansion on any unaccepted prerequisite, critical safety or tenant leak, stale identity/source/model approval, unresolved high-severity defect, privacy deadline risk, unknown effect/charge, exhausted approved budget, missing operator coverage or failed recovery/observability threshold. The actual alert/latency/cost/response thresholds must be accepted beforehand; synthetic local observations do not define them.

1. Preserve the original command/request/send/publication reference and masked receipt. Pause new mutations for uncertain outcomes. Current authorized status/receipt/purpose reads can continue. Reading an unrelated refused receipt or refreshing a page does not resolve the original unknown state.
2. Use existing authorized negative policy controls and applicable suspension/kill switches to stop new admitted work. They do not cancel an already dispatched provider effect or erase a measured/unknown charge. No enable, arbitrary dispatch or quota-reset endpoint is introduced by this runbook; resumption needs the existing separately reviewed policy/authority process.
3. Keep deterministic/manual assessments and permitted immutable report reads available where their own permissions and safety requirements still pass. A withdrawn/deleted image or revoked actor must not regain access just because fallback exists.
4. Recheck current authority and inspect the original durable outcome before retry. Reconcile publication, coupon allocation, model/provider charges, notifications and deletion marks through their existing services. Do not mint a new command key to escape uncertainty, replay a provider effect blindly or clear a privacy hold to restore service.
5. If rollback is authorized, drain/restrict new work and promote only the recorded compatible prior image/configuration. Preserve SQL/object histories, permission revocations, charges, coupons, publication receipts and privacy journals. Do not run destructive down migrations or restore an older backup directly into an admitted runtime. Any restore must replay deletion marks under accepted custody before reopening access.
6. Record root cause, measured outcomes and named operator/owner acknowledgment. Resume only after a fresh authorized review of policy/source/rights/evaluation, incident disposition, budgets, privacy barriers, target health and candidate compatibility. A local receipt or operator acknowledgment alone does not prove an external effect resolved.
